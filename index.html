Afọpẹ́fólúwa Òjó writes fiction and builds spatial prototypes, between Lagos and Amsterdam.

She has taught a machine to tell Egungun from Gelede, sent poems found on the internet to subscribers one at a time, and written her father into an interactive poem. Her stories have appeared in Overland, Lolwe and Necessary Fiction, been nominated for the Pushcart Prize, and longlisted for the Commonwealth Short Story Prize.

She is building ita.ng, an innovation lab bridging the gap between art and technology. Everything she makes lives under // àlà //.

Represented by Alexa Stark, Writers House.
